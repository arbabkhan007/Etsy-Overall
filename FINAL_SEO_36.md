# NovalityStore - the title + tag set to freeze until ~10 Feb 2027

Validated: 36 listings, 13 tags each, every tag <=20 characters, no phrase used twice anywhere in
the shop, no trademark-as-headline tags, no year stamps outside the one listing where the year is the
product. **Paste, do not retype.** `FINAL_SEO_36.csv` is the spreadsheet version of this file.

## The five rules this set was built under

1. **First phrase = the exact query.** Etsy weights the leading words hardest and mobile cuts at about
   40 characters, so the product type has to survive the cut.

2. **One phrase, one listing.** Your previous document had `beginner crochet` on 8 listings and
   `google sheets budget` on all 4 budget files. Etsy shows whichever of your own listings already has
   the sales, so the copies wasted a slot for four months. Zero collisions now.

3. **Tags carry intent, titles carry compatibility.** 'Excel and Sheets' moved into titles where it also
   earns the click; it left the tag boxes, where 'google sheets' only rented browse traffic.

4. **A freeze is a season bet.** Seasonal crochet files keep all 13 season tags - their next window is
   October 2027 and you want them at full strength for the eight weeks that matter. Files that peak in
   January (budgets, bookkeeping, lifestyle, wedding) spend tail slots on New Year intent instead, so
   December does not eat the whole set.

5. **Claims you cannot prove do not go in the text.** `AIA billing` is out (American Institute of
   Architects' mark). Anything on the verify list below stays out of the paste until you counted it.

## Paste order and the freeze protocol

- Do all 36 in one sitting, today or tomorrow. Every extra day of drift is a day of the Q4 ramp you
  do not get back, and Etsy re-matches on the next crawl anyway.

- Expect impressions to wobble for 3-10 days, then rebuild. That is normal after a title change and it
  is NOT a signal to edit again - the single most expensive mistake in a freeze is reacting to the dip.

- The freeze covers titles and tags only. Your sale, your prices, your ads, your gallery images and
  video stay adjustable, and that is where the money in this window actually comes from.

- One exception: listing #14 (Halloween) is dead inside your window. Leave it as written, do not
  'optimise' it, and revisit in August.
