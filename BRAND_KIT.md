# NovalityStore brand kit
### banner · icon · logos · section headers · listing templates

Generated by `make_brand_assets.py` — everything is drawn in Pillow, not painted by a model.
That's deliberate: AI-made brand marks look handsome in a preview and collapse at the 16px
Etsy actually renders, and I can't look at an image to catch it. Every asset here is
parametric, so a change is a one-line edit and a re-run, not a re-roll.

```bash
python3 make_brand_assets.py     # writes brand/ (20 files)
```

## The rules that make this a brand and not clip-art

**One accent, used once.** Forest green is the ink; terracotta (`#C2643F`) is *only* ever on the
thing you want clicked — CTA, underline, sale. The moment terracotta decorates something else, the
click signal dies. This is the whole system in one sentence.

**Type sizes are computed, never chosen.** `fit_font()` shrinks a line until it fits the safe width.
Hard-coded sizes are how text gets clipped at 2000px while looking perfect in a preview — the worst
class of bug, because you only find it after publishing.

**Square-safe by construction.** Etsy search shows a centre-cropped square even though you upload 4:3.
Every word in the listing templates lives inside the middle 1500px. The `preview_search_square_*`
files exist so you check the crop *before* uploading, not after.

**The banner never states a discount.** `Sale ends in 5:45:54` on a banner is a countdown to nothing
by the time the page is cached, and a stale "25% off" on a permanent fixture is how you train buyers
to wait. Discounts go in the sale tile and the listing, where they can be swapped in a day.

**No faces, no stock photography, no gradients.** A digital-goods shop sells competence; a photo of
a stranger's kitchen sells nothing you can verify. Cream + grid + one accent reads like a studio.

## What to do with them

1. Upload banner + `shop_icon_160.png` in Shop Manager → Edit shop.
2. Add the two section banners.
3. For each of the 6 new listings, open the matching template, drop your own photo into the beige
   panel, change the title line and the three facts, export at 2000×1500. **Facts must be true for
   that file** — "13 TABS" on a 9-tab sheet is the axolotl review waiting to happen.
4. Keep the file name pattern `listing-<slug>-v1.png`. Version the images like you version the PDFs.

## Fonts

Drawn in DejaVu (serif for the wordmark, sans for everything else) because that's what's on the box.
For anything you design by hand, the free equivalents that keep the same character:
**Playfair Display** or **Fraunces** in place of the serif, **Inter** in place of the sans.
Never use a third family, and never italicise the wordmark.
