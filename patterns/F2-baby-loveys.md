## Materials

- Cotton DK in cream or oatmeal for the blanket (cotton survives washing better than acrylic, and
  a baby gift that pills once looks cheap), plus DK scraps for the animal heads.
- Hook 3.0 mm for the head, 3.5 mm for the blanket field so it drapes rather than stands up.
- Fibrefill, approx 12 g per head.
- Embroidery needle and 30 cm of dark brown embroidery thread per face.
- Optional: a printed care card (the template is in the bundle's `start-here.pdf`).

## Gauge

10 sc × 12 rnds = 2.5 cm with 3.0 mm in the round; 10 sc × 11 rnds = 2.5 cm with 3.5 mm for the
blanket. The blanket deliberately gauges looser than the head - a rigid lovey does not curl into a
baby's arm.

## Yarn and substitutions

Buy by millimetres, not by a hook letter: "size 7" is 4.5 mm in the United States and 3.0 mm on an
old English hook, and that single mix-up is why so many free-pattern reviews say "tiny".

| what the pattern calls for | US shelves | UK shelves |
|---|---|---|
| Worsted / 10 ply, 5.0 mm | Lion Brand I Love This Cotton, Wear It, Red Heart Super Saver | Patons Astra, Sirdar Snuggly Baby Crofter, Stylecraft Special DK doubled |
| DK / 8 ply, 4.0 mm | Bernat Premium DK, Wool-Ease DK | Stylecraft Special DK, Paintbox Cotton DK, King Cole Baby DK |

For a baby item, cotton or a soft acrylic blend, and nothing with a halo. Mohair, brushed yarns and
non-superwash wool shed, and a lovey that sheds near a sleeping face is the one substitution not to
make, however nice the skein looks.

## Care, and the card to print with the gift

Hand wash cold with a drop of mild detergent, squeeze in a towel, dry flat. No machine, no tumble,
no iron on the head. Check the seams before each wash and re-sew a loose one the same day: a lovey
is only as safe as its tightest join. Give the lovey with the card below inside the gift wrap so the
parents get the instructions without asking you.

```
CARE CARD - cut along the dotted line, slide under the ribbon

  Crochet by [your shop name] - made to be chewed, dragged and washed
  Hand wash cold, dry flat, no tumble dryer, no iron on the face.
  Check the seams before each wash. If a part loosens, take it off
  before the next sleep, not after.
  Surface wash only. Remove before unsupervised sleep.
```

## P01 · Shared blanket (worked flat-in-the-round as a hexagon)

R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4  (2 sc, inc) x6 — 24
R5  (3 sc, inc) x6 — 30
R6  (4 sc, inc) x6 — 36
R7  (5 sc, inc) x6 — 42
R8  (6 sc, inc) x6 — 48
R9  (7 sc, inc) x6 — 54
R10  (8 sc, inc) x6 — 60
R11  (9 sc, inc) x6 — 66
R12  (10 sc, inc) x6 — 72
R13  (11 sc, inc) x6 — 78
R14  (12 sc, inc) x6 — 84
R15  (13 sc, inc) x6 — 90
Finish: work one even round in blo, then a border of (2 sc, ch 2, sk 1) around. A hexagon reads as
"made on purpose" next to a square, and it drapes over a car-seat handle without bunching.
Two-tone option: change colour at R7 and carry the second ball up the inside of the last three
rounds - three rounds of float is safe, six starts to pull the fabric.

## P02 · Shared head shell

R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4  (2 sc, inc) x6 — 24
R5-R9  sc around — 24
R10  (2 sc, dec) x6 — 18
R11  (1 sc, dec) x6 — 12
R12  dec x6 — 6
Finish: stuff before R10, then cinch R12 closed. Sew the head to the blanket centre through the
blo of R6 so the join sits in the middle of the hexagon and does not ride up. Advanced note for the
back of the PDF: leave R12 open and work the blanket directly from the head's 6 remaining stitches
- same result, no sewing, and it is the version the 5-star reviews of the big shops describe.

## The four loveys

### F01 · Bunny

Work P02 in cream. Ear (make 2): R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (1 sc, inc) x3 — 9
R3-R8  sc around — 9
Inner ear pink: work 4 rows of 5 sc in pale pink and sew flat inside the front of each ear.
Face: satin-stitch two 3 mm eyes at R6, five stitches apart, and a tiny Y nose below.
Finish: pinch the top of each ear over 1 stitch before sewing so they fold instead of standing.

### F02 · Bear

Work P02 in honey. Muzzle: R1  MR, 4 sc — 4
R2  (inc) x4 — 8
R3  sc around — 8
Ear (make 2): R1  MR, 4 sc — 4
R2  (inc) x4 — 8
R3-R4  sc around — 8
Finish: sew the muzzle over R7-R8 with nose in the middle, ears at the head's R3 corners. Bear is
the one to photograph first: it is the safest gift and the most likely to be bought by someone who
has never made a lovey.

### F03 · Elephant

Work P02 in pale grey. Trunk: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2-R9  sc around — 6
Ear (make 2): R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4  (2 sc, inc) x6 — 24
R5  sc around — 24
Finish: stuff the trunk only through the last 3 rounds and bend it up at the base before sewing -
a straight trunk hangs over the blanket like a hose and is the number-one way an elephant lovey
looks wrong in a photo. Flatten each ear, sew at R3 so it fans out behind the head, and cut the
back loop of the ear's outer edge twice to let it lie flat.

### F04 · Lamb

Work P02 in cream. Ear (make 2): R1  MR, 4 sc — 4
R2  (inc) x4 — 8
R3  sc around — 8
Legs of fleece: after R9, work one round of 3-dc bobbles into each stitch for the woolly cap
around the crown only, then finish R10-R12 as P02. Face: a 12-st smooth oval sewn over the front,
or simply leave the head untextured and fringe 20 cream strands at 1.5 cm around the crown -
the fringe version is the one people actually copy from the photo, because it takes four minutes.

## Finishing

- Weave every tail back through three stitches on the inside and add nothing else: no glue on a
  baby item, because glue goes hard and then scratches.
- Wash the finished lovey once on a 30° cycle before you photograph it. Cotton blooms, the fabric
  softens, and the photo stops looking like a stiff board.
- Trim the pile on the blanket with small scissors if you dropped a stitch - a bobble looks like a
  flaw at 1:1 on a screen.
- If you offer the finished item as an upcharge (many lovey sellers charge $45-60 made), say in the
  listing that it is hand-washed and that safety eyes are not used at all.
