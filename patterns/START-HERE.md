## Before you cast on (2 minutes, saves a whole re-do)

1. **Read the gauge block and hook it.** 5 cm square, counted, before you commit to 24 motifs. If
   your square is 3 stitches wide, go up a hook. If it is 15, go down. Do not "make the tension
   tighter" — the fabric will be board-stiff and the toy will not round out.
2. **Buy hooks by millimetres.** "Size 7" is 4.5 mm in the US and 3.0 mm on an old English hook.
   Every size complaint in this niche is that mix-up, not the pattern.
3. **US and UK terms are both printed in each file.** The abbreviation table is not decoration.
4. Mark your rounds with a safety pin or a scrap of contrast yarn. Multiplied rounds like
   `(2 sc, inc) x6` are where an even row lost becomes visible three rounds late.

## If a piece comes out wrong

- **Cupping or hexagonal edges** — you are decreasing at the end of a repeat instead of spreading
  them, or you joined mid-round where the pattern says continuous.
- **Too many stitches** — an `inc` you worked at the round start twice. Count at the end of every
  round for the first five rounds, then trust the pattern.
- **Fabric too small for the photo** — you used DK where it says worsted, not a smaller hook.
- **Name letters crooked** — work them before seaming, on the un-sewn leg, with the chart pinned
  beside you rather than open on a phone at 40% zoom. Print the chart page.
