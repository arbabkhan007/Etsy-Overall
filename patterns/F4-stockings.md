**Product:** one shared stocking, 5 front-panel motifs, an A-Z letter chart for names, 2 sizes.
**Sell as:** `Crochet Christmas Stocking Pattern, 5 Farmhouse Stockings With A to Z Name Chart, 2 Sizes, Monogram Holiday Stocking PDF Download` — list $15.99, 25% off, realise $11.99.
**TEST_STATUS:** untested draft — crochet one stocking and one letter before publishing. The letter

**BABY_SAFE:** no — these are decor; the dog stocking (below) should say so too.

The name chart is the product. A plain stocking pattern competes with a 23,900-review seller at
$10 and a $1.62 newcomer; a stocking pattern that lets someone put BENNETT on the cuff in stitch
has no direct comparison, which is why a brand-new listing in this search can hold $12.95 flat.

## Materials

- Aran or double-strand DK in the ground colour (approx 90 g per stocking), plus scraps for cuff,
  heel and motif.
- Hook 4.5 mm (US 7); 5.0 mm for the foundation chain so the toe does not pucker.
- Tapestry needle; 2.5 cm wooden toggle per stocking for hanging.
- For the name: embroidery floss in one colour, or the same yarn in a contrast.

## Gauge

12 sts × 14 rows = 5 cm (2 in) in sc, 4.5 mm hook, held double where the pattern says so.
Finished stocking: P01 as written = 27 cm (10.5 in). Small version = stop the leg at Row 24 = 20 cm.
Ornament version = follow M01 of the advent garland, which is this same shape worked on 9 stitches -
mention that in the listing, it turns one buyer into two sales.

## Yarn and substitutions

Stockings are a worsted-and-a-large-hook project on purpose: you want a fabric with body so the
cuff stands up and the name does not sink. Buy by millimetres, not letters - "size 7" is 4.5 mm in
the United States and 3.0 mm on an old English hook.

| what the pattern calls for | US shelves | UK shelves |
|---|---|---|
| Aran, 5.0-5.5 mm | Lion Brand Wash It, Vanna's Choice, I Love This Cotton Worsted | Patons Lumsden, Sirdar Snuggly Double Knit held double |
| Worsted doubled to reach aran | Red Heart Super Saver x2 | Stylecraft Special DK held double |
| Contrast for cuff and motif | Bernat Home Deco Cotton | Paintbox Cotton Aran, Hayfield Bonus Aran |

Cotton or a cotton blend for a stocking that will be stuffed and handled every December; wool
stretches under the weight of the presents and never quite returns.

## Care

Machine wash cold in a pillowcase, or hand wash, and dry flat - a hung wet stocking grows two
centimetres at the toe and the name goes crooked. Do not tumble: the turned cuff curls and the
embroidered letters shrink into the fabric. Wash before the first fill, once, so any red cuff yarn
is off your white ground before December 25 rather than on it.

## P01 · The stocking (flat, seamed, worked toe to cuff)

Row 1  MR, 8 sc — 8
Row 2  inc, sc 6, inc — 10
Row 3  inc, sc 8, inc — 12
Row 4  inc, sc 10, inc — 14
Row 5  inc, sc 12, inc — 16
Rows 6-10  sc 16 — 16
Row 11  dec, sc 12, dec — 14
Row 12  dec, sc 10, dec — 12
Row 13  dec, sc 8, dec — 10
Rows 14-26  sc 10 — 10
Row 27  inc, sc 8, inc — 12
Row 28  inc, sc 10, inc — 14
Row 29  inc, sc 12, inc — 16
Rows 30-38  sc 16 — 16
Row 39  ch 1 and turn, 16 sc in blo — 16
Rows 40-44  ch 1 and turn, sc 16 — 16
Finish: fold Rows 39-44 forward on the R39 back loops to make the turned cuff, then seam the toe
and the back with a ladder stitch, matching the increases either side of the ankle so the foot sits
square instead of splaying. Work the motif and the name onto the leg before the back seam is closed
- you cannot hold a stuffed stocking steady while you embroider a W.

## The five fronts

Each is an appliqué worked separately and pinned onto the leg between Rows 14 and 26, centred 2
stitches from each edge. Sew only the outer edge; a fully-sewn motif telegraphs every bump.

### F01 · Pine tree

Row 1  MR, 3 sc — 3
Row 2  inc, sc 1, inc — 5
Row 3  inc, sc 3, inc — 7
Row 4  inc, sc 5, inc — 9
Row 5  inc, sc 7, inc — 11
Row 6  ch 1 and turn, sc 11 — 11
Finish: work one row of (3 sc, dec, 3 sc) — 9 at the base to narrow the trunk, then a 4-ch hanger.
In cream ground with a green tree, the cuff in red: that is the farmhouse colour plan, and every
photo in this niche that sells uses a light ground rather than a dark one.

### F02 · Reindeer

Face: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4  sc around — 18
Ear (make 2): R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2-R3  sc around — 6
Antlers: freeform — ch 6 with 5 sc, forking at Row 11 with ch 3, sc 2.
Finish: embroider the eyes after sewing so the disc does not pucker. Nose in red, one French knot
through both layers with a 3 mm stitch on the back to lock it.

### F03 · Snowman

Body: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4-R5  sc around — 18
R6  (1 sc, dec) x6 — 12
Hat brim: Row 1  ch 14, 13 sc across — 13
Rows 2-3  ch 1 and turn, sc 13 — 13

Crown (separate piece): Row 1  ch 10, 9 sc across — 9
Rows 2-4  ch 1 and turn, sc 9 — 9
Finish: sew the crown at the R6 neck line and the brim just below it; scarf as a 20-ch with 10 sc,
wrapped and tacked. A scarf with no tails looks like a collar, so leave 3 cm hanging.

### F04 · Poinsettia

Centre: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
Petal (make 8): Row 1  ch 6, 5 sc along one side, ch 1, 5 sc back — 10
Row 2  ch 1 and turn, dec, sc 6, dec — 8
Row 3  ch 1 and turn, dec, sc 4, dec — 6
Finish: flatten each petal and sew the tip of four to the front disc centre in a cross, then four
between them behind, so the flower has depth instead of looking like a asterisk in yarn.

### F05 · Gingerbread man

Body: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4-R6  sc around — 18
R7  (1 sc, dec) x6 — 12
R8  dec x6 — 6
Arm (make 2): R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2-R4  sc around — 6
Leg (make 2): R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2-R3  sc around — 6
Finish: this one is 5 cm across, not 10 like the others - a full-size gingerbread on a stocking reads
as a child's stocking, and the buyers making one per family member will otherwise get the scale
wrong in their first attempt. State the size on the image, not only in the text.

## Name chart

The full A-Z in `patterns/charts/AZ-name-chart.md`, generated by `make_charts.py` as a fixed mosaic grid per letter
(8 wide x 10 tall at the time of writing - take the number from the generator's header, not from here) - do not hand-type these, and do not "tidy" the generator's output by hand.

How the buyer uses it, in the order that stops the messages: work the name in the chart at 1 stitch
per square on the leg before seaming, either as duplicate stitch over the fabric or as mosaic with
a second yarn held along the wrong side (carry, do not float more than 4 stitches on a stocking that
will be handled). Short names go across the cuff; long names go down the leg in two lines. If the
buyer wants 12 letters of name and the chart is 9 rows tall, that is what the smaller size is for.

## Finishing

- Weave tails as you change colour rather than at the end; a stocking has four colour areas and
  finishing all the tails after seaming is where people give up.
- Block over a can of beans or a rolled towel, pins at the cuff and toe only. Steam, do not press.
- If you offer the toggle as a separate physical add-on listing, keep the digital file free of it.
