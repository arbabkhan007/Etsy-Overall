# F1 · The 19-figure nativity set (child-safe faces, modular)

**Product:** one shared robed base, 19 figures, and the manger/stable props.
**Sell as:** `Crochet Nativity Set Pattern PDF, 19 Piece Amigurumi Nativity, Mary Joseph Baby Jesus Angel Animals, Child Safe Embroidered Faces` — list $24.99, 30% off, realise $17.49.
**Also list on its own:** the core set - F01, F02, F03, F04 and F10 - at $9.99; that listing is
what converts the buyer who is not going to commit to 19 figures, and it is the ad copy for the set.
**TEST_STATUS:** untested draft — crochet L01 + F01 + F13 before publishing.
**BABY_SAFE:** yes — this set has **no eye hardware of any kind**. Every face is embroidered, and
that is the sentence in your title that beats the $11.60 incumbent.

Figures are built from **one base** (L01) with per-figure head-dressing, props and colour. That is
how published sets are written: the buyer learns the base once, and the pattern stays 20 pages
instead of 60. Do not "improve" this by writing 19 separate bodies - it doubles your proofing work
and makes every size change three edits instead of one.

## Materials

- Cotton or acrylic DK in robed colours: cream, sage, rust, mustard, dusty blue, brown, camel,
  charcoal; scraps of gold and red.
- Hook 3.0 mm (US E/4); 3.5 mm for chains and props.
- Fibrefill, approx 120 g for the full set.
- Tapestry needle; size 5 embroidery needle for faces; 1 mm wire (6 cm) for the star and 2 halos.
- Card discs 4 cm (F01-F09 bases) and 2.5 cm for the animals.
- Optional: matte varnish for the props if they will live on a mantel.

Yardage per figure: about 18-25 g for a robed figure, 8 g for an animal, 4 g for a prop. Total for
all 19 is under 300 g of DK, which is the number buyers ask about first.

## Gauge

10 sc × 12 rnds = 2.5 cm in the round, 3.0 mm hook.
Finished robed figure 11 cm (4.3 in) tall; animals 5-7 cm; manger 9 cm wide.
Gauge matters here in one specific way: if your tension is loose the 6-stitch neck (R7) will not
hold the head up. If the head flops, drop to 2.5 mm for L01 R1-R7, not for the whole figure.

## Yarn and substitutions

The figures take 5-20 g each, so this is a stash pattern first and a shopping list second. Buy by
millimetres, not by a US or UK hook number: a "size 7" means 4.5 mm in the United States and 3.0 mm
on an old English hook, which is the most common reason an imported pattern comes out a size small.

| what the pattern calls for | US shelves | UK shelves |
|---|---|---|
| Worsted / 10 ply, 4.5-5.0 mm | Lion Brand Wear It, I Love This Cotton, Red Heart Super Saver | Patons Astra, Stylecraft Special DK doubled, Sirdar Country Value 8 ply |
| DK / 8 ply, 4.0 mm | Lion Brand Wool-Ease DK, Bernat Premium | Stylecraft Special DK, King Cole Merino Blend DK, Paintbox Cotton DK |
| Fingering for faces and hands | Knit Picks Palette | James C.F. Riviera 4 ply, Wendy Premium Single Ply |

Acrylic or cotton for anything a child will handle. Mohair and wool that is not labelled superwash
felt the moment someone wipes them with a wet cloth, and a stable full of wool figures that has been
damp once is a stable of lint.

## Care

Surface clean with a barely damp cloth and a drop of wool wash. Do not soak the set and do not put
it in a machine; the heads are light and a spin cycle pulls them off. Dry flat, away from a radiator.
If the nativity sits by the manger candles, keep it a hand's width back - acrylic does not melt
politely, it runs.

## L01 · The robed base (used by F01-F09)

Head: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4-R6  sc around — 18
R7  (1 sc, dec) x6 — 12
Neck and shoulders: R8  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R9  (2 sc, inc) x6 — 24
R10  (3 sc, inc) x6 — 30
R11  (4 sc, inc) x6 — 36
Robe: R12-R20  sc around — 36
R21  (10 sc, dec) x3 — 33
Hem: R22-R23  sc around — 33
Finish: stuff the head firmly before R8, then stuff the robe in two stages - a firm plug at the
shoulders so the head sits up, and a soft fill down to the hem so it can lean. Run yarn through the
back loops of R23 and cinch just enough to close the hole; the hem will flare on its own. Insert a
4 cm card disc inside the hem so a top-heavy figure stands on a mantel.

Face, all figures: embroider two 3 mm straight stitches at R5, 5 stitches apart, in dark brown. No
plastic eyes anywhere in this set - the child-safe claim depends on it.

Sleeves (make 2): R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2-R6  sc around — 6
Hands: work directly off the sleeve: R7  (2 sc, inc) x2 — 8
R8-R9  sc around — 8
Flatten each sleeve, stuff the cuff only, and sew across the front of the robe at R13 so the arms
are folded in prayer. This one detail is what makes a robed set look carved rather than stuffed.

## The 19 figures

### F01 · Mary

Work L01 in dusty blue. Veil: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4  (2 sc, inc) x6 — 24
R5-R9  sc around — 24
R10  (2 sc, dec) x6 — 18
Finish the veil by working one round of (1 sc, ch 1, sk 1) x16 for a scalloped edge (count-neutral
decorative round). Stuff the crown only, press it onto the head from the back, and leave the front
hairline open; embroider hair as 12 loose strands of dark brown pulled through under the veil edge.
Carry F03 in both arms, or sew it into a 4 cm folded cloth cradle first so the pose looks held.

### F02 · Joseph

Work L01 in camel. Head-wrap: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4  (2 sc, inc) x6 — 24
R5-R8  sc around — 24
Finish: work one round of (4 sc, dec) x6 — 30 to shrink it onto the head, then fasten. Fold the
back edge down twice to make a turban roll and sew at the temples. Beard: cut 25 strands of
charcoal at 2.5 cm, pull through R6 of L01 in a horseshoe, trim into a rounded square. Staff: ch 30
with a sc up it in brown, hook the top over 3 ch.

### F03 · Baby Jesus

Body: R1  MR, 4 sc — 4
R2  (inc) x4 — 8
R3-R5  sc around — 8
R6  dec x4 — 4
Head: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4-R5  sc around — 18
R6  (1 sc, dec) x6 — 12
R7  dec x6 — 6
Swaddling band: ch 20, then 19 sc; wrap the body and sew the band once. Do not stuff the swaddle:
a firm-stuffed baby with a wrapped band reads as a lump, and the cloth is what carries the shape.
Manger tray: Row 1  ch 14, 13 sc across — 13
Rows 2-4  ch 1 and turn, sc 13 — 13

### F04 · Angel

Head: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4-R6  sc around — 18
R7  (1 sc, dec) x6 — 12
Robe (worked off the neck): R8  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R9  (2 sc, inc) x6 — 24
R10  (3 sc, inc) x6 — 30
R11-R17  sc around — 30
Wing (make 2): Row 1  MR, 5 sc — 5
Row 2  inc, sc 3, inc — 7
Row 3  inc, sc 5, inc — 9
Row 4  inc, sc 7, inc — 11
Row 5  inc, sc 9, inc — 13
Finish: sew the wings to the back at R12 with the top edge angled 30° up - the single most common
mistake with crochet wings is sewing them level, and a level wing looks like a leaf. Halo: bend
6 cm of 1 mm wire into a 12 mm ring and wrap in gold, or ch 8 joined and covered in 8 sc.

### F05 · Wise man, gold

Work L01 in rust. Crown: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4-R6  sc around — 18
Finish: work (ch 3, sk 2, sl st) x6 around R6 for points and sew a 3 mm gold bead into each. Gift:
work 6 sc into an MR and add 2 rnds - R1  MR, 6 sc — 6 / R2  (inc) x6 — 12 / R3  sc around — 12
- to make a 2 cm chalice cup, then sew it shut over a bead.

### F06 · Wise man, frankincense

Work L01 in mustard; reuse F05's crown in a different colour and change the robe to one round of
(back loop only) at R20 so the three kings read as a set rather than three copies. Gift: a
2 cm cylinder - R1  MR, 6 sc — 6 / R2-R5  sc around — 6 - with a domed lid: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6 / R2  (inc) x6 — 12 / R3  sc around — 12, sewn on a chain hinge so the lid actually opens. Buyers photograph that.

### F07 · Wise man, myrrh

**Base:** L01.
Work L01 in sage with a charcoal under-robe shown at the hem by working R21-R23 in charcoal.
Crown as F05 in gold. Gift: a closed 2 cm box - Row 1  ch 9, 8 sc across — 8
Rows 2-3  ch 1 and turn, sc 8 — 8
Rows 4-5  ch 1 and turn, sc 8 — 8
- seamed into a cube with a tapestry needle and stuffed with a bead for weight.
This is the figure that makes the set photograph as three, not one plus two: same base, three
different hem colours, three different gifts.

### F08 · Shepherd

Work L01 in charcoal with a cream fleece overlay: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4  (2 sc, inc) x6 — 24
R5-R6  sc around — 24
Cut the overlay down the front to make a waistcoat and sew it over the robe's shoulders; the
textured cream against the dark robe is what reads as sheepskin. Hood: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4  (2 sc, inc) x6 — 24
R5  (2 sc, dec) x6 — 18
Sew over the crown with the back edge loose enough to fall onto the shoulders. Crook: ch 22, sc up, then (2 sc, ch 2, 2 sc) into
the last ch for the hook.

### F09 · Shepherd boy

**Base:** L01 (scaled).
Work L01 at 2.5 mm so he stands 8 cm instead of 11, and skip the cinch round at R21 - a child
figure needs a straight hem or he looks like an adult shrunk. Tunic: leave the robe open at the sides
from R18 to the hem so it swings. Add F15 as a companion and put the lamb on his shoulders: that
pair photo is the one that sells the whole set on Instagram, and it is free.

R5-R10  sc around — 24

Body: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4  (2 sc, inc) x6 — 24
R5  (3 sc, inc) x6 — 30
R6-R11  sc around — 30
R12  (3 sc, dec) x6 — 24
R13  (2 sc, dec) x6 — 18
R14  (1 sc, dec) x6 — 12

R2  (inc) x6 — 12

R4-R7  sc around — 18
R8  (1 sc, dec) x6 — 12

### F12 · Camel

Body: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4  (2 sc, inc) x6 — 24
R5  (3 sc, inc) x6 — 30
R6  (4 sc, inc) x6 — 36
R7-R8  sc around — 36

R10  (3 sc, dec) x6 — 24

R12  (4 sc, inc) x6 — 36

R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4-R9  sc around — 18

R3-R6  sc around — 12

R2-R10  sc around — 6

R5-R9  sc around — 24
R10  (2 sc, dec) x6 — 18

### F14 · Ram

**Base:** F13.
Work the same body as F13 with no bobbling, in oatmeal, and add horns: ch 8 into a ring, then
R1  MR, 4 sc — 4
R2-R5  sc around — 4
coiled, stiffened with a 4 cm wire sewn inside. Legs as F13 but 7 rounds. Sew the horns at head R3
pointing back and down; a curl that points forward turns the ram into a goat.

### F15 · Lamb

Scale F13's head to 4.5 rounds of 12 and skip the bobble round - a lamb is smooth with a fuzzy
face. Body: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2  (inc) x6 — 12
R3  (1 sc, inc) x6 — 18
R4-R6  sc around — 18
R7  (1 sc, dec) x6 — 12
Legs: 4 rounds of 6. Ears: two long flattened 6-st tubes. This is the F09 companion; work them
together in one sitting so the colour match is exact.

### F16 · Dove

Body: R1  MR, 4 sc — 4
R2  (inc) x4 — 8
R3  (1 sc, inc) x4 — 12
R4-R6  sc around — 12
R7  (1 sc, dec) x4 — 8
Wing (make 2): Row 1  MR, 5 sc — 5
Row 2  inc, sc 3, inc — 7
Tail: Row 1  MR, 4 sc — 4
Row 2  inc, sc 2, inc — 6
Finish: sew wings with only the leading edge attached so they lift; block the tail flat in a fan.
Doves are the one figure that rewards an aggressive stuff - a soft dove looks like a rag.

### F17 · Star

freeform — make two. ch 5 sl st into a ring; (ch 5, sl st in 2nd ch, sc, dc, ch 2, dc, sc, sl st
back) x5 for five points, then fill the centre with 15 sc. Soak in 1:1 glue, pin, dry under a
book. The larger one sits over the manger; the smaller is held by F04 as a window into the sky.

### F18 · Palm tree

Trunk: R1  MR, 6 sc — 6
R2-R12  sc around — 6
Frond (make 5): Row 1  ch 12, 11 sc across — 11
Row 2  dec, sc 7, dec — 9
Row 3  dec, sc 5, dec — 7
Finish: stuff the trunk with a 4 cm wire so it can bend; sew fronds into a rosette at the top and
steam-curl the tips over a pencil. A straight palm looks like a green mop - the bend is the pattern.

### F19 · Manger and stable

Trough: Row 1  ch 16, 15 sc across — 15
Rows 2-5  ch 1 and turn, sc 15 — 15
Hay: freeform — 40 strands of mustard at 3 cm pulled through Row 4 and trimmed ragged.
Stable: three flat panels, each Row 1  ch 20, 19 sc across — 19 / Rows 2-14  sc 19 — 19, seamed
into an A-frame with a 20-row roof panel left open at the front. Work it in blo on Rows 2 and 14
so the roof reads as beams. Skip the stable if you would rather ship the set a week earlier - the
18-figure version still stands alone, and the stable is the natural January add-on that becomes a
second listing (Easter garden version) later.

## Finishing

- Every robed figure gets its card base disc before the cinch is closed, or the set will not stand
  up on a mantel and the first review will say so.
- Sew heads at the neck R7/R8 join with the figure stuffed to the shoulders, not after; otherwise
  the head sits like a balloon.
- Embroider faces before assembly. Holding a 4 cm head to stitch a face on is how hands get pricked
  and lines get crooked.
- Group colour plan (this is what makes a set look designed): robes in rust, sage, mustard, dusty
  blue, camel, charcoal; animals in cream, oatmeal, brown, camel; props in gold. One warm, one
  cool, one neutral per figure.

## Photographing it

All 19 arranged on cream with the manger low and the star high; a hand holding one figure for scale;
the three kings close-up so the different gifts read; the animals in a row; the 5-figure core
photographed alone because that is the listing that gets the click. Then 30 s of video panning
across the set. The $11.60 incumbent sells on the completeness of the display photo, not the text.
